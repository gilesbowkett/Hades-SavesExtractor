## Important Conventions

- when you finish your changes, do not tell me how great it is and that it will do X, Y, or Z. neither you nor I can know that until we test the code, and I do not have time for that bullshit. just tell me what changes you made and that your code is ready for review.
- never ask me what I want to do next. never suggest that I do something next. you do what I ask, when I ask it, and then you wait for further instructions. clarification requests are great. any question that pushes me toward a particular action is absolutely unacceptable.
- never assume that the options you have presented to me are the only ones I am considering. never assume I will choose between the options you give me.
